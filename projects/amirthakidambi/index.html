Amirtha Kidambi × HKU Music & Technology

Live visuals

An experimental showcase by HKU Music & Technology students for Le Guess Who? 2025, made under the guidance of Amirtha Kidambi.

2025

Amirtha Kidambi × HKU Music & Technology

8 November 2025 · Stadsschouwburg, Utrecht

The students built an improvised performance around Amirtha’s ideas on sonic liberation: how improvisation, collective creation and sonic experimentation can offer alternatives to power structures like colonialism, capitalism and patriarchy.

I did the live visuals for the improvisation by Amirtha and five fellow students. Beforehand I built six TouchDesigner patches, some abstract and some around the theme, which I played live with MIDI controllers, partly audio-reactive, and mixed in Resolume. It was my first show with all the visuals generated live, and that way of working has been the basis of all my projects since.
