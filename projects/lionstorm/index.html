Lionstorm

Since 2024 Live visuals

Since 2024 I’ve worked with rap duo Lionstorm (@by_lionstorm) on the visual side of their shows: VJing live during their performances and editing their showfiles.

2025

She Was Forgiven

11 December 2025 · Muziekgebouw aan ’t IJ, Amsterdam

An evening retreat presented by Lionstorm, with Rama, Iceboy Violet, Izzy Spears and Lionstorm live.

I was the VJ for the night: I mixed the duo’s own show visuals live, turned the other artists’ press photos and videos into visuals with live effects, and made a name visual for each artist with my analogue video gear.
