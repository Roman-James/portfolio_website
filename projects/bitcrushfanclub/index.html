Bitcrush Fanclub

Since 2026
Member, VJ

Bitcrush Fanclub (@bitcrushfanclub) is an NL/BE digi-art collective I’m part of. We organise events together, from club nights to expos, and support each other’s art.

I’m the VJ at our shows and help shape the collective’s visual identity; every week we work out the concept for the next event together. I use a Kinect and cameras to turn what happens in the room into live, reactive visuals.

2026

Sewer.fm

11 September 2026 · De Sering West, Amsterdam

sewer.FM, a mysterious pirate radio station broadcasting from the sewers below Amsterdam, teamed up with Bitcrush Fanclub for a live radio show: DJs, live music, mini performances, an expo and fashion, all gloopcore, rat-style and digital slime pop. With Pip Pen, Keynu, NANO, BSoD, Orchestra and DJ Smob. The location changed to De Sering West before the night.

Cyberlesque: one-year anniversary

5 September 2026 · Teatro, Utrecht

Bitcrush Fanclub’s first anniversary: a free night of DJ sets and theatrical performances by the collective’s members and special guests. It took burlesque as its starting point, playing with the tension between humour and sensuality, as an answer to a digital world that pushes you to expose every detail of your life.

Pixieclub

12 June 2026 · Popradar, Den Haag

Popradar’s Radarcafé turned into PIXIECLUB, a dark digital fairy-tale forest, as if the audience had been pulled out of the parallel world inside their computer. Performances by Luid, ms. palace, nano and </alice>, with merch, an art expo and decor.

I brought back the butterfly flocking simulation I built for Tentacles.

Photos by Bart van Maarseveen (49)

Cultuurcentrum Datamosh

3 June 2026 · Het Bos, Antwerp

A free night at Het Bos in Antwerp: live music, an art expo and live visuals, somewhere between hyperpop, avant-garde beats and tech nostalgia. With Luid Met Jou, Orchestra, Zegenmij@25, Sio’s Orange Core, NANO and Pirko (DJ).

I designed the poster and expanded my patch with datamosh effects and an early-internet look.

Photos by Bart van Maarseveen (83)

Simplosion

25 April 2026 · Musicon, Den Haag

The release night of Implosion, the debut album of Sio’s Orange Core, about simulated lives and early online communities, and how we find each other in systems built around individualism and self-optimisation. Live sets by Sio’s Orange Core, NANO, </alice> and Jongentje van Vlees, DJ sets, an expo and a market.

My visuals built on the Internetcafé patch, with a Sims look and a plumbob (the green Sims crystal) that followed the artist in real time.

Photos by Romeo Zeph (9)

Internetcafé

12 March 2026 · ACU, Utrecht

A multidisciplinary art and music evening at ACU: experimental electronic acts, DJs, VJs, an expo and an art market. With nano, Sio’s Orange Core, Luidmetjou, Orchestra, Pip Pen and zegenmij@25, DJ sets by yamaha siren and djnaive, and a clothing sale by Roxy Spikkel.

Our first self-organised event, and the first time I used a Kinect: I rendered the artists live in 3D as point clouds, bringing the digital into the physical room. I’ve used it at every show since.

Photos by Bart van Maarseveen (34)
