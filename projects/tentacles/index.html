Tentacles

30 May 2026 · De Helling, Utrecht

I did the visuals for Ivy Lab’s two-hour farewell-tour set, with my own TouchDesigner patch. The only brief was the theme Bugs: I built a flocking simulation of butterflies, added tentacles to the 3D scene and leaned into glitches in the look and effects. The visuals ran through Resolume onto a TV wall built by Roel Weerdenburg.
