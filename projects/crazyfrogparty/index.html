Crazy Frog Party

YEARS: 2023 – 2025
ROLE: Everything: organising, visuals, music
WHERE: My house, Amsterdam

Crazy Frog Party is a Crazy Frog themed party I throw in my own house. I do everything myself, from organising and decorating to the visuals and the music: DJ sets, live visuals, a freestyle battle and more surprises.

Crazy Frog Party 4: Senior Year

13 September 2025 · Amsterdam

“Every frogling has to grow into a frog… but not before one last crazy splash!” The Senior Year edition, with a Frog Pass as ticket. The night had a Mario Krat race, a rap battle, and sets by Lennart de Piloot b2b Premmetje_P, </alice>, Nano, bladerdag, zegenmij@25 and DJ Flipphone.

Crazy Frog Party 3

19 May 2024 · Amsterdam

Frogs, music, visuals and many more surprises: KZL: De Gameshow, a Mario Krat tournament, a rap battle, spiritual frog yoga and a Frog King ceremony, with De Blaaskikkers, Kenny, DJ Syddy, DJ Kaakschaats, DJ Flipphone, TCP (live), The Whispering Frogs, Boemie b2b LilZwerfRat and DJ Mango Ice.

Crazy Frog Party 2

9 September 2023 · Amsterdam

Live performances, DJ sets, live visuals and more surprises: DJ Kaakschaats, TCP (live), DJ Mango Ice, DJ Boemie, DJ Flipphone and a rap battle.

Crazy Frog Party 1

3 June 2023 · Amsterdam

The first Crazy Frog Party: DJ sets by Kaakschaats, Flipphone, Boemie and Mango Ice, a Kahoot quiz and a rap battle.
