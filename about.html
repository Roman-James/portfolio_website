About

I'm a Dutch music technologist based in Amsterdam, and I recently graduated in Music & Technology from the HKU University of the Arts Utrecht. I design and perform live visuals: translating music into image, so that sound and image become one total experience.

My visuals are generated live from real-time data: cameras, a Kinect, audio input, BPM and the crowd itself. I work in very different contexts, from club nights and festivals to concert halls and installations, always from the perspective of a music technologist.
