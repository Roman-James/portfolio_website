About

I'm a Dutch music technologist based in Amsterdam, currently studying Music & Technology at the University of the Arts in Utrecht. My main areas of focus are creative system design (programming with music) and klankontwerp (experimenting with sounds in varying live contexts). I also have a keen interest in the intersection of sound and visuals and their immersive potential.
